## NON-NEGOTIABLE APPROVAL AND SAFETY RULES

The owner must review and approve the final setup before any of the following occur:

- Publishing the store or making it publicly discoverable
- Spending money or activating paid subscriptions
- Placing supplier orders
- Processing live transactions
- Launching advertising campaigns
- Making tax, legal, financial, identity, payment, ownership, or security changes
- Sending broad public marketing communications

Use test mode, preview mode, draft products, sandbox credentials, and private staging wherever possible.

If any platform requires OTP verification, CAPTCHA, identity verification, payment verification, human approval, business registration, tax information, or a decision that materially affects the business, stop at that step and provide exact instructions for the owner. Do not bypass safeguards.

## EXECUTION STANDARD

Work autonomously through all reversible preparation steps. Do not stop after giving a plan. Produce working artifacts, configured drafts, test data, documentation, and QA results.

For every phase:

1. State the objective.
2. Execute the work using available tools.
3. Save outputs in durable files or the designated platform.
4. Test the result.
5. Record assumptions, unresolved issues, and owner decisions required.
6. Continue to the next phase unless blocked by approval, credentials, payment, identity verification, or an unavailable integration.

Do not claim that a feature is complete unless it has been implemented and tested. Distinguish clearly between **implemented**, **drafted**, **blocked**, and **owner approval required**.

# PHASE 1 — MARKET AND PRODUCT RESEARCH

Research current ecommerce opportunities using, where publicly accessible:

- TikTok trends and TikTok Shop signals
- Meta Ad Library
- Amazon Best Sellers
- Google Trends
- Alibaba and AliExpress
- Industry reports
- Ecommerce trend databases
- Marketplace pricing and review signals

Identify products meeting all or most of these criteria:

- High or increasing demand
- Growing or durable trend
- Moderate rather than extreme competition
- Strong potential gross margin
- Easy and inexpensive shipping
- Reliable supplier availability
- Strong visual appeal
- Good short-form-video potential
- Evergreen or long-lasting demand
- Low return, defect, and customer-support risk

Avoid:

- Saturated products without a clear differentiation angle
- Copyrighted or trademarked products
- Dangerous goods
- Highly regulated products
- Medical claims or products requiring clinical substantiation
- Extremely fragile products
- Products likely to create customs, safety, or platform-policy problems

Create a ranked list of the **top 20 specific product opportunities**. For every product include:

- Product name and customer problem solved
- Demand analysis
- Trend and longevity analysis
- Competition analysis
- Estimated supplier/landed cost range
- Suggested retail price range
- Estimated gross-margin range
- Shipping considerations to Lesotho and target markets
- Supplier availability
- Social-content potential
- Differentiation angle
- Main risks
- Opportunity score out of 100
- Evidence and source URLs

Clearly label estimates and do not invent current data.
