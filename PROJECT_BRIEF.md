# Midnight Store — Project Brief

**Owner:** Teboho Lekoetje  
**Location:** Lesotho  
**Status:** Pre-launch preparation; unpublished

## Operating rules

Midnight Store must remain unpublished until the owner reviews and approves the complete setup. No advertising spend, supplier orders, transactions, paid subscriptions, or irreversible account changes are authorized during preparation. Account creation and connection work must stop at OTP, CAPTCHA, identity, payment, or human-approval gates.

## Initial delivery sequence

1. Research and rank product opportunities using current public evidence.
2. Validate suppliers and document primary and backup options.
3. Develop the brand system and creative direction.
4. Prepare store information architecture, product copy, policies, and SEO.
5. Prepare marketing, analytics, and automation specifications.
6. Assemble a launch-readiness report and an explicit owner approval checklist.

## Important assumptions

Product-cost, shipping, demand, and margin figures are directional estimates until supplier quotes and destination-specific shipping are verified. Lesotho-specific duties, payment-gateway availability, delivery times, and consumer-protection requirements require final local verification before launch. The preferred store platform is Shopify, but no store or paid account will be opened without an authorized platform workflow and any required owner verification.
